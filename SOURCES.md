# Source and authorship

Academic work by Federico Pucci at Universidad Nacional de La Matanza. Authorship of the selected Java exercises and analysis models was confirmed by the author during publication review. Course case studies and exercise prompts remain teaching material.

Only selected diagram exports are published. The native `.vpp` archives also contain unrelated exercises and class templates, so those mixed containers are kept locally. Exported images preserve Visual Paradigm attribution.
